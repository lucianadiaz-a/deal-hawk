
## 0. Set context
Intro speech

## 1. Compass 
1) What are the top 1–2 business goals for the next 6–12 months?  
2) What’s the #1 operational bottleneck holding that back?  
3) If we fixed one workflow this quarter, what would move revenue/margin/speed most?

## 2. Last-time story 
4) Walk me through the **last time** this bottleneck happened.  
   - Trigger → steps → tools → handoffs → outcome  
5) Where did it **stall** or require **manual effort**?  
6) What **workarounds** do people use (spreadsheets, copy/paste, double-checking)?  
7) Where do people **lack confidence** in the data or decision?

## 3. Quantify 
8) How often does this happen (per day/week/month)?  
9) How much time does it consume (per instance + per week)?  
10) What’s the cost of delay/failure? (missed deals, margin leakage, inventory risk, customer impact)  
11) What have you tried already, and why didn’t it stick?

## 4. Value unlock + constraints
12) If you got this time back, what higher-value work would you focus on? What blocks that today?  
13) Non-negotiables: compliance, data access, vendor/retailer constraints, risk tolerance  
14) Define POC success that would allow you to unlock stakeholder funding and full budget to investing in complete product development.
